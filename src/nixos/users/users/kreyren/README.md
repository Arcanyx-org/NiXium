# Kreyren

## Statement on Piracy

Work In Progres.....

This is my opinion which does not represent the Arcanyx-org Project.

I use 3rd party launchers such as HydraLauncher which is a steam alternative with built-in BitTorrent which are often affiliated with piracy (though they have legitimate usecases as well).

I do not support piracy as such practice is taking away resources from game developers who need those to get a decent salary and improve the game, but as someone who has over 7000 games across multiple steam accounts and sank a large sum of money into Valve already I often feel scammed by bad actors in the industry who make a game that takes few minutes to complete, lacks content and is over-priced while often employing grinding tactics to get over the 2 hours play time of the user to make it impossible to refund afterwards.

Valve enables these bad actors through their (In My Humble Opinion) badly design refund policy which floods the platform with half-assed games that are not worth my time and money.

Additionally the Steam Client for both Windows and Linux has been a constant source of frustration and pain for me as it feels like it's backend has been written by under paid pre-schoolers which is not meant to be an insult to the developers behind Steam, but rather pointing out the experience I had with the client to show the bad culture and quality assurance practices, unless this wasn't obvious already after the https://github.com/ValveSoftware/steam-for-linux/issues/3671 fiasco..

Additionally to that Valve's commisions which currently takes 30% is terrible for starting or small developer studios, i do not criticize the 30% as Steam is a big platform and if they actually promote the developers (through massive deals like steam sales etc..) and help them grow then 30% is fair deal in my humble opinion, but taking such a high amount from a starting game developers is very demanding and often leads to them having to go into debt to be able to work on the game. Valve already tried to fix this with a tiered system which is great, but instead of temporarely going to 20% it should go close to 5-10% or even offer incentives and resources to help such developers to start their own gaming studio.
